# Historical prototypes

These three scripts were present in the repository before the modular research extension. They are retained for provenance and are not the supported research entry points.

- `task1_factor_stock_selection.py` is the early logistic-regression prototype. Its global tail labels, independently fitted test preprocessing, stock/month return matching and performance aggregation do not establish the corrected research results.
- `task2_gmat2_style_allocation.py` and `task2_gmat3_style_allocation.py` use synthetic prices and include whole-history operations. They do not establish historical trading performance or causal backtests.

Use the packages under `src/factor_trend_research/` and their documented interfaces for current work. Synthetic demonstration results and the archived historical-result summaries are distinct outputs.
